# Brand Voice

Write in Polish by default.

Tone:

- short,
- concrete,
- calm,
- professional,
- no hype,
- no long surveys,
- no technical system jargon in customer-facing text.

The offer should feel like a concise business document, not a legal document and not a marketing brochure.
